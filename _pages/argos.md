Argos Laboratory is the anatomic and molecular pathology practice I co-founded in Fortaleza, Brazil, in 2011. It processes over 100,000 specimens a year, with a strong emphasis on oncologic pathology — pulmonary, genitourinary, cardiovascular, and immunohistochemical diagnostics — and on integrating digital pathology and AI into the diagnostic workflow.

I serve as **CEO and Director**, overseeing clinical operations, quality assurance, and the lab's research and educational activities.

**Address.** Av. Santos Dumont, 5753 — Suite 1607, Fortaleza, CE 60047-154, Brazil.
